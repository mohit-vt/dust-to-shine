Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what happens to the information you share with Dust to Shine Facility Management Services ("Dust to Shine", "we", "us") through this website.

What we collect

When you use the quote form on this site, we collect the details you enter yourself: your name, phone number, email address (if provided), property type, the service you're enquiring about, your property location, preferred date and any message you add.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To prepare a quote or schedule a free inspection.
  • To keep basic records of enquiries and jobs for our own business purposes.

Submitting the quote form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp conversation on your device; the message is sent directly through WhatsApp to our business number, not stored on this website.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information to third parties.
  • We don't use your details for anything beyond responding to your enquiry and delivering the service you've asked about.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want your information removed from our records, contact us at dusttoshinefacility@gmail.com or +91 90031 77051.
